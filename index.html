Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you accept insurance?

Yes, we work with most major insurance providers. Bring your card and prescription and we'll confirm coverage at the counter.

Can I transfer my prescription from another pharmacy?

Absolutely. Bring your current prescription bottle or ask us to call your previous pharmacy directly — we'll handle the transfer for you.

Do you offer free delivery?

Yes, we offer free local delivery within our delivery radius. Ask at checkout or when placing your order by phone.

Do I need a prescription for everything in your shop?

No. Items like EviSoft Cream and Islim are available over the counter. Products such as Tretinoin Gel require a valid prescription — our pharmacist can advise you.

What are your operating hours?

Monday–Friday 10:00 AM–8:00 PM, Saturday 9:00 AM–6:00 PM, and we're closed on Sundays.

How can I reach the pharmacist directly?

Call us at +254701467309 or email junripharmacy@gmail.com — we're happy to answer medication questions over the phone.
